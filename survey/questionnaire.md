# Survey questionnaire

> This is the core question set used to study how pharmacies manage stock and sales.
> **Edit it to match the questions you actually asked** before publishing.

**Area:** Sanirakhra (Shonir Akhra), Dhaka
**Who answers:** the owner or the person who runs the counter

| # | Question | Answer type | CSV column |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which method do you use to manage stock and sales? | App / Manual (register, khata) / Both | `method` |
| 2 | If you do not use an app, why not? (tick all that apply) | Lack of knowledge, Lack of staff, No computer, Cost, Power or internet problems, Data safety worries, Small shop - no need, Other | `reasons` |
| 3 | How many people work at the counter? | Number | `staff_count` |
| 4 | Would you use a free app that works offline and in Bangla? | Yes / No / Maybe | `would_adopt` |
| 5 | Anything else the owner wants to say | Free text | `notes` |

## Tips for collecting answers

- Use a code instead of the shop's name: `P01`, `P02`, `P03`... Keep the list that links codes to shop names on paper or in a private file.
- Ask the same questions in the same order every time.
- Record the answer in the owner's own words in `notes`; those quotes are often the most valuable part.
